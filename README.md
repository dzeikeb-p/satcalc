# SatCalc

the calculator that won't let the dollar lie to you — you punch in forty-two, and up crawls its sat-ghost from underneath, uninvited, the truth breathing through the fiat lie, until the exchange rate stops being math and starts being sight. no lesson, no quiz, no man at a chalkboard — just the habit you already have, calculating, and a second number riding shotgun on every first one, thousands of times, until the skull knows sats the way it knows miles or ounces or the weight of a body. it is a mirror that happens to also add.

## The mechanism

people already hold the calculator up to money when they're deciding whether they can afford the thing. that's the whole crack in the door. slide the sat number in beside the dollar number, ambient, unrequested, always burning in the corner of the eye, and the brain does the rest on its own time, unsupervised — the way you learn a smell before you learn its name. gate the sat number behind a tap and you've killed it; it has to be wallpaper, or it's nothing.
